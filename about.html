Meet the Specialist

Andy Bongar
Equipment Connection Specialist

With over 35 years in the imaging and copier industry, Andy Bongar has built a reputation as one of the most connected and trusted equipment specialists in the country. His deep knowledge of the market — from manufacturer specs to dealer dynamics — makes him an invaluable partner for anyone buying or selling imaging equipment.

Andy founded Imaging Connection to do one thing exceptionally well: bridge the gap between dealers with equipment to sell and dealers who need it. No middlemen, no wasted time — just direct, professional connections that move inventory and accelerate business.

Based in Westlake Village, California, Andy works with dealers nationwide, leveraging a network built over decades of hands-on industry experience. Whether you're offloading a fleet or sourcing a single unit, Andy brings the expertise, contacts, and drive to get it done.
